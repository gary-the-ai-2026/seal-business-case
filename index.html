What has to be true

Three assumptions keep every number clean.

1 · Business use

100% business use

A 12-week logbook substantiates the percentage. The figures shown assume full business use; if it is 70%, every saving scales to 70%.

2 · Ownership

Bought by the trust

SnowArk Unit Trust, the GST-registered trading entity, owns it. Routing the purchase through the company would lose the GST credit entirely.

3 · Rate basis

Deduction at your rate

Savings priced at your 37% bracket plus 2% Medicare. If your income pushes you to the 47% band, the saving grows to roughly $24,950.
